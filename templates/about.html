ABOUT HEALTHPREDICT

Smarter Healthcare Appointment Analytics

HealthPredict is a machine-learning-based healthcare analytics application designed to estimate the probability of a patient missing a scheduled medical appointment.

Try Prediction

Why HealthPredict?

Missed healthcare appointments can create unused appointment capacity and make appointment management more difficult. Identifying appointments that may have a higher probability of no-show can help healthcare teams consider additional reminders or confirmation steps.

HealthPredict uses patient and appointment characteristics to generate a probability estimate. The result is presented as a decision-support signal rather than a definitive statement about whether a patient will attend.

HOW IT WORKS

From Patient Information to Prediction

The application follows a simple machine-learning prediction workflow.

01

Patient Information

The user enters patient characteristics, health information, neighbourhood, appointment timing, and SMS reminder information.

02

Feature Processing

The application prepares the submitted information in the same feature structure expected by the trained machine-learning pipeline.

03

Risk Prediction

The trained model generates a no-show probability, which is then interpreted using the application's classification threshold and risk bands.

MODEL INFORMATION

XGBoost Prediction Pipeline

The application uses a trained XGBoost pipeline to estimate the probability of an appointment no-show. The trained pipeline receives the same feature structure used during model development.

The current application uses a tuned classification threshold of 20% for identifying potential no-show appointments.

SYSTEM FEATURES

What HealthPredict Provides

Probability Score

Displays the estimated probability that an appointment may result in a no-show.

Risk Categories

Results are visually classified into Low, Medium, and High risk categories based on the predicted probability.

Dataset-Based Neighbourhoods

The neighbourhood dropdown is populated from the actual healthcare appointment dataset rather than relying on manually entered values.

Interactive Web Interface

Users can enter patient information, submit a prediction, and immediately view the model-generated result.

Important Notice

HealthPredict is a machine-learning project intended for educational and analytical purposes. Its probability estimates should not be treated as medical diagnoses or definitive predictions of individual patient behavior. Healthcare professionals should apply appropriate judgment when interpreting model outputs.
